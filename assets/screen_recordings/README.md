# Screen recordings (scene 3 of every commercial)

Real captures of jobcommand.app — never AI-generated UI. Name each file after
its scene id: `CK-01-03.mp4`, `CK-02-03.mp4`, `CK-03-03.mp4`. Any aspect ratio
works (it is centre-cropped to 1080x1920); record in portrait on a phone for
the best result. Clips shorter than the narration loop, longer ones are trimmed.

What each must show (see `manifests/jobcommand_v2.csv`, `visual_prompt`):

- **CK-01-03** — customer opens booking link, picks a slot, pays deposit, job lands on the schedule.
- **CK-02-03** — invoice created from the finished job on a phone, customer pays, paid confirmation.
- **CK-03-03** — dispatch board with every truck on the live GPS map, job assigned to a tech.

Use demo data: no real customer names, addresses or phone numbers on screen.
