# NFL Benter test with real closing lines (nflverse games.csv)

## Bottom line (read this first)

**Nothing beats the 52.38% break-even out of sample, for either spreads or totals.**  Zero picks are issued.  The model is a worse predictor than the closing line, and the blend correctly collapses to 'trust the market'.

* **Spread, holdout 2023-25:** model-only all games 47.8% on n=836 (95% CI 44.5-51.2%); model-only |x|>=T* 48.6% on n=109 (95% CI 39.4-57.9%); fitted blend ex-ante 54.5% on n=11 (95% CI 28.0-78.7%); fixed w=0.3 blend 49.6% on n=254 (95% CI 43.5-55.7%); market-only coin flip = 50%.
  Incremental-information slope of model-minus-line on outcome-minus-line in 2023-25: b = -0.244 (s.e. 0.165) -> indistinguishable from 0 (b=0 means the line already contains everything the model knows).  Fitted blend weight: b=0.0130, implied w ~ 0.11.
* **Total, holdout 2023-25:** model-only all games 51.5% on n=848 (95% CI 48.2-54.9%); model-only |x|>=T* 50.0% on n=186 (95% CI 42.9-57.1%); fitted blend ex-ante no picks fired; fixed w=0.3 blend 52.4% on n=227 (95% CI 45.9-58.8%); market-only coin flip = 50%.
  Incremental-information slope of model-minus-line on outcome-minus-line in 2023-25: b = +0.087 (s.e. 0.191) -> indistinguishable from 0 (b=0 means the line already contains everything the model knows).  Fitted blend weight: b=0.0000, implied w ~ 0.00.
* Straight-up check (moneyline no-vig): the fitted blend gives the model a NEGATIVE coefficient; blend log loss is no better than the market alone.
* The best-looking cells in the sensitivity tables are noise buckets (n=11 to ~250, CIs span break-even).  With ~8 looks, a lone pass at p<0.05 would still be expected by chance.
* Leakage test (leak_test.py): corrupting every result from 2023 week 10 onward changes no earlier prediction (max |diff| = 0).

Caveats: nflverse closing lines (not the Dabble / Underdog / bet365 lines you will actually see); 2026 lines in the file are whatever is currently loaded (Week 6 lines are early); starting QBs in the file are projected, injuries/inactives not checked; no weather in 2026 forecasts; -110 assumed for payouts.

Generated by benter.py / nfl_model.py in this directory (re-runnable).  Data: nflverse/nfldata games.csv, 2007-2026.

## Design (what was fit where)

* **Model (no market inputs):** margin = team rating + QB rating (online, Elo-style SGD) + online home field + rest differential; total = team offence + team defence + QB-offence ratings + dome/wind/cold terms.  Margin point forecast is an 80/20 blend of the Elo+QB margin and the margin implied by the offence/defence model (weight fit on 2010-19).  Week-batched walk-forward: every game is predicted before any result of its own week is used.  2007-09 = rating burn-in; hyper-parameters tuned on 2010-19 only (Nelder-Mead on MSE).  Residual sd used for probabilities: margin 13.40, total 13.49.
* **Blend + rule:** fit on 2020-22.  **Holdout:** 2023-25 never touched until the final evaluation; everything frozen.
* **Lines:** nflverse `spread_line` (home margin expected) and `total_line` are the closing lines in the file.  Pushes are dropped.  -110 break-even = 52.38%.
* **Market-only baseline:** the closing line implies 50% for each side (log loss 0.6931); no-vig probability from the posted odds is also shown.
* Weather: the totals model uses wind/temp when the file has them (history).  For 2026 un-played games they are blank, so weather is not in the 2026 total forecasts.

Fitted margin params: `{"Kt": 0.0507, "Kq": 0.0242, "rho_t": 0.4444, "rho_q": 0.9665, "hfa0": 0.5, "hfa_lr": 0.0023, "qnew": -3.6456, "c_rest": 0.0662, "cap": 100.0}`

Fitted totals params: `{"Ko": 0.0441, "Kd": 0.0343, "Kqo": 0.0132, "rho_o": 0.3, "rho_d": 0.5069, "rho_qo": 1.0, "kL": 0.0, "qnew_o": -0.2644, "c_dome": 1.4016, "c_wind": -2.5793, "c_cold": -0.6552, "L0": 22.0}`
