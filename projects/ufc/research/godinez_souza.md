## Matchup analysis (ANALYST ESTIMATE, built on the sourced stats above)

**Range and striking.** Souza has about 2" more reach, boxing-first, accurate (57%), explosive, and has real one-shot power for the division (head-kick TKO, KD on Jauregui). Godinez is the busier, tidier mid-range striker by defense (62-63% vs 53%) but lower accuracy (49%), and she lost a decision to Hill, who beat Souza too, so both are beatable by volume. Godinez has never been finished by strikes in the sources found; her only stoppage loss is the Suarez submission, so Souza's knockout path is real but not the likeliest.

**Wrestling vs takedown defense.** The core mismatch is about 3 landed takedowns per 15 min (42-47% accuracy) for Godinez against a 53-54% takedown defender who is also the one with 13% takedown accuracy herself. Souza gives up a lot on the mat and fence if Godinez commits to level changes. Caution: Godinez's grappling is not elite. Jandiroba stuffed her game and controlled her, Dern out-controlled her, and Suarez took her back twice. Souza has a Jauregui RNC on her sheet and was a Luta Livre/grappling-origin fighter (Wikipedia), so she is not helpless from the bottom, but she was also submitted by Silva.

**Pace and cardio.** Godinez averages 13:21 per fight and has gone 3 rounds in most UFC fights; she paces for 15 minutes. Souza's 9:29 average is dragged down by early finishes, but her last two decisions were close, and her power is concentrated early. If Souza has not hurt Godinez by the end of R1, the pace edge shifts to Godinez.

**Game plans (inferred, no quotes found).** WKX/Jauncey: pressure to the fence, mix jab-level changes with kickboxing combinations, chain takedowns, win rounds with top control and volume. Carioca/Bezerra: keep distance with the jab and straight, use the longer reach, sprawl and punish entries with counter knees/head kicks, and look for the early finish or a backup scramble sub.

**Paths.**
- Godinez (about 62-66%): decision by takedowns plus control and volume (most likely), or a late top-position stoppage/sub (less likely given 3 finishes in 20 fights).
- Souza (about 34-38%): early distance striking/counter on entries and a first-round hurt-and-finish (needs only one clean shot), or a close decision won by defending takedowns and out-landing at range. Her recent form (two straight wins, a R1 KO) is her best since 2024.

**Lean: Godinez**, mainly because of the takedown mismatch and durability, but I would not call it a lock. My fair price is about -170 to -185 (62-65%), which is cheaper than your 1.42x (-238, 70.4%) and cheaper than the -215/-255 books. So at 1.42x the favorite looks fully priced to slightly rich, and Souza at about +180-190 is closer to fair value. Souza has a real path (power plus Godinez's recent submission loss), not just a nominal one.

**Goes the distance?** Likely, about 65-70% (my estimate): Godinez has 11 decisions in 14 wins and has not finished anyone since 2023; ats.io projects a decision; Sports Gambling Podcast expects 15 minutes. Main risk to the distance: Souza's R1 power (finishes in 2 of her last 4 wins) and Godinez's top-position submission chances.

**Prop thoughts (Godinez).** Takedowns: about 3 landed per 15 min on a 53% TD defender. If the fight goes the distance and she is committed, 2-4 is reasonable; lines of 1.5-2.5 would be the natural zone. Risks: if Souza stuffs the first few and Godinez switches to striking, or Souza finishes early, the count craters (variance is high; she also landed 2/3 vs Dern but was stuffed 7/9 times by Jandiroba). Significant strikes: 4.2-4.5/min x 13:21 average = about 56-60 per fight at her average length, 63-68 over a full 15 minutes, but takedown-heavy rounds lower strike counts (control time doesn't add strikes). Treat any strike line near 55-60 as roughly fair, not an edge. Fight-time props: Godinez average 13:21 vs Souza 9:29 suggests a full fight is more likely than not, but no line was found.
