# Data Sources and Signals for an Information Edge on WNBA Player Props, and the Legal/Ethical Limits

*Research date: 2026-10-10. Method caveat: in this environment, direct page fetching (WebFetch/curl) to vendor and league sites was blocked by the egress proxy (DNS/CONNECT failures for the-odds-api.com, wnba.com, sportsgameodds.com, frontofficesports.com). Every finding below comes from search-engine summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the full pages. Vendor claims (coverage dates, prices, "free historical") are flagged as **unverified** where they come from the vendor itself, a competitor, or a directory site. Verify against live vendor docs and pricing pages before buying anything.*

## 1. When are WNBA injury reports and starting lineups published, how fast do prop markets react, and does faster news give an edge?

### Takeaway
The WNBA's official injury report is due by 5 p.m. local time the day before a game (1 p.m. local on game day for the second game of a back-to-back). After that it updates roughly every 15 minutes up to tip. Enforcement is weak: teams have disclosed ruling a star out under two hours before tip and received only a fine or a warning. Starters have no league-mandated pre-tip deadline. Referee assignments post around 9 a.m. ET on game day. Anecdotes show books pulling props within minutes of credible news, with stale lines lingering at slower books. No study measures how fast the WNBA prop market reacts, so any "speed edge" is a short, unquantified window, mostly at slower or smaller books.

### Cited Findings
**Official injury report: rule and timing**
- WNBA policy: teams must give a participation status for any player whose availability may be affected by **5 p.m. local time the day before a game**. For the **second game of a back-to-back**, the information is due by **1 p.m. local time on game day**. Reports are updated continually through the day. — [WNBA Injury Report (official page)](https://www.wnba.com/webview/wnba-injury-report)
- After the initial deadline, reports are **updated every 15 minutes** closer to game time. Status categories cited in coverage: out, doubtful, questionable, active. — [Front Office Sports (AP-based coverage, 2026)](https://frontofficesports.com/article/fever-get-warning-no-fine-over-caitlin-clark-injury-report/); [Front Office Sports, May 21 2026](https://frontofficesports.com/?p=236632)
- "Probable" is also in use. Caitlin Clark was listed "probable (back)" for the **32nd time** in the 2026 season ahead of the Fever's return from the FIBA World Cup break. This shows that a low-severity designation carries little information. — [Sports Illustrated (Fever), 2026](https://www.si.com/wnba/fever/caitlin-clark-gets-familiar-injury-designation-upon-fever-world-cup-return-01m2tc29w3gf)

**Enforcement and late disclosure (which drives late-news risk)**
- **Sept 2024:** the Las Vegas Aces were fined an undisclosed amount for not listing A'ja Wilson's ankle injury the day before a game vs. the Liberty. She was not on the report released the night before, arrived in a boot, and was ruled out several hours before tip. — [Front Office Sports](https://frontofficesports.com/article/fever-get-warning-no-fine-over-caitlin-clark-injury-report/); [CBS Sports/HoopsHype](https://hoopshype.games.cbssports.com/wnba/news/aja-wilson-injury-update-aces-star-out-vs-liberty-after-injuring-ankle-has-no-long-term-concerns/)
- **May 20, 2026:** Caitlin Clark (back) was ruled out vs. Portland. The Fever disclosed it **less than two hours before tipoff**, and she had not been on the report a day earlier. The league issued a **warning, not a fine**. Coach Stephanie White said "She's healthy, we're not managing anything," while a reporter described a "strategic management plan." — [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/sports/caitlin-clarks-injury-status-may-prompt-wnba-review-11975772); [Newsweek](https://newsweek.com/sports/wnba-issues-warning-to-fever-after-controversial-caitlin-clark-move-11980954); [Daily Gazette/AP](https://www.dailygazette.com/ap/sports/wnba-warns-the-fever-for-failing-to-report-caitlin-clark-s-injury-on-time/article_142617d7-81d4-4d65-ac44-ade2d0ffa353.html); [SI timeline](https://www.si.com/wnba/caitlin-clark-injury-timeline-when-fever-superstar-could-return)

**Starting lineups**
- RotoWire reports that **the WNBA does not require starting lineups to be submitted before tipoff**, so starters can be unknown until shortly before the game. RotoWire publishes "expected" lineups about **24–30 hours before tip** and marks them "confirmed" only when an official WNBA source provides them. It flags "late scratches." — [RotoWire WNBA Lineups](https://www.rotowire.com:443/wnba/lineups.php); [RotoWire (confirmed lineup view)](https://ww1.rotowire.com/wnba/lineups.php)

**Referee assignments**
- WNBA game-day referee assignments (crew chief, referee, umpire, alternate) are posted on **official.nba.com at about 9:00 a.m. ET each game day**. Crews are three on-court officials plus one on-site alternate. — [NBA Official: Referee Assignments](https://official.nba.com/referee-assignments/); [WNBA 2020 playoffs officials release](https://pr.nba.com/2020-wnba-playoffs-officials/); [WNBA 2018 Playoffs officials](https://official.nba.com/wnba-2018-playoffs-officials). (The newest WNBA-specific confirmation found dates from the 2021 playoffs, so 2026 practice is unverified.)

**How fast prop markets react (anecdotal/practitioner evidence only)**
- In the Draymond Green (NBA) case, reported by ESPN around January 2022, **FanDuel removed Green props within minutes** of The Athletic's report. Other books were slower, bettors took advantage, and **most of the liability came from bets placed in the last 10 minutes before tip**. — [ESPN Chalk](https://www.espn.com/chalk/story/_/id/33043002)
- Action Network (a commercial education piece, not research) describes three stages: books take markets off the board, reopen with adjusted lines, then sharps push prices to equilibrium. It says many major books run **automated tools that scrape social media and news feeds** for keywords. These claims are unverified. — [Action Network](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/how-injuries-affect-betting-lines-a-guide-to-market-movement)
- Practitioner consensus is that a news edge exists but is narrow and short-lived. A March 2026 piece argues "You aren't going to beat Vegas to the punch," and that the better angle is spotting book or public overreaction. — [Bleacher Nation, March 2026](https://www.bleachernation.com/betting/2026/03/28/march-madness-injuries/); [RotoStreetJournal (NFL), 2025](https://www.rotostreetjournal.com/2025/10/29/nfl-injury-report-player-props-week-9-best-fantasy-and-betting-picks/)
- A CBS Sportsline analyst (2020) said books "over-correct for injury a little bit," citing one example. — [Yahoo Sports AU](https://au.sports.yahoo.com/one-sneaky-player-prop-trend-171738094.html)

**Academic evidence on market efficiency (no WNBA prop-specific study found)**
- Paul & Weinbach (2014), "Market Efficiency and Behavioral Biases in the WNBA Betting Market" (2007–2012 sides/totals data): simple betting strategies **do not earn statistically significant returns**. WNBA bettors, like NBA bettors, prefer the best teams, especially on the road, but fading popular sides was not significantly profitable. — [search summary referencing the paper; repository: Kennesaw/Coastal digital commons](https://digitalcommons.coastal.edu/finance-economics/13)
- Kochman & Goodwin, "Market Efficiency and the Women's NBA," tests whether an "emerging stage" market lacks the participation needed for efficient pricing. Results were not retrievable. — [Kennesaw State Digital Commons](https://digitalcommons.kennesaw.edu/facpubs/2419)
- An NFL moneyline thesis (Claremont, 2025) found pre-kickoff line moves reflected sportsbook responses to sharp action rather than injury news. — [Claremont thesis](https://scholarship.claremont.edu/cmc_theses/3975)
- NBA totals showed an early-season bias that markets corrected only partly. — [ETSU, "Learning, Price Formation and the Early Season Bias in the NBA"](https://dc.etsu.edu/etsu-works/17780)

**WNBA prop-market structure (practitioner/marketing sources, unverified)**
- One betting newsletter says WNBA prop lines come mostly from US books that "seem less accurate than they could be," and that aggressive WNBA bettors may get limited. — [Probly newsletter](https://probly.beehiiv.com/p/wnba-betting-obvious-edge)
- Stat Pick says the WNBA market is hard because the data is sparse, and predicts the easy edges will disappear within a few years. This is vendor marketing. — [StatPick blog](https://www.statpick.ai/blog/wnba-props-edge)
- Action Network reported that the WNBA prop market got sharper through a season. — [Action Network WNBA props](https://www.actionnetwork.com/wnba/wnba-player-props-betting-forecast-kahleah-copper-brittney-sykes-natasha-cloud)
- ETR (writing about NFL props) names Caesars and FanDuel as the sharpest domestic prop books, and says Caesars typically allows at least $500 limits for sharp accounts. — [Establish The Run](https://establishtherun.com/understanding-the-current-ecosystem-of-nfl-player-props/)

### Inferences
- The informative windows are (a) the **5 p.m. local day-before report**, (b) **~9 a.m. ET referee postings**, (c) game-day report updates, especially the last 90–120 minutes, and (d) **starter confirmation near tip**. Because starters are not required in advance and the league enforces lightly (a warning in 2026), the highest-value late information concentrates in the final 1–2 hours. That is also when books are most alert and limits are lowest.
- A small modeler is unlikely to win a pure latency race against books that auto-scrape feeds plus sharp syndicates. The more durable edge is **better conditional modeling of what the news means**: redistributing minutes and usage to teammates when a player is out or limited, and pricing "probable/questionable" correctly given team-specific history. Teammate props at slower books may reprice more slowly than the injured player's own props, which are pulled first. This is a hypothesis from the Draymond anecdote, not a measured fact.
- The 2026 Clark episode (32 "probable" listings; a surprise late scratch) suggests building **team-level designation reliability features**: how often each team's "questionable" or "probable" listings end in DNP or limited minutes.

### Gaps
- No timestamped study of WNBA (or NBA) **player-prop** repricing after injury reports or lineup news was found. Latency figures beyond the single Draymond anecdote are unavailable.
- An official WNBA rule on when starters must be submitted (e.g., an NBA-style pre-tip submission to officials) could not be confirmed. RotoWire says none exists.
- The full text of the 2025/2026 WNBA injury reporting policy (official PDF) could not be read. The deadline wording comes from a search summary of the official webview page.
- Whether WNBA referee assignments are still posted at 9 a.m. ET in 2026 was not confirmed (latest confirmation is from 2021).
- Kochman & Goodwin's WNBA efficiency results were not retrievable.

## 2. What historical WNBA player-prop odds archives exist, with what coverage, books, closing lines and prices (2025–2026)?

### Takeaway
The most clearly documented self-serve option is **The Odds API**. It offers WNBA player props from US books only, with historical "additional markets" (including player props) from **May 3, 2023** in **5-minute snapshots**, on paid plans. Third-party listings put tiers at roughly **$30–$249/month**. SportsGameOdds, SportsDataIO, OddsJam and OpticOdds all claim historical odds or closing lines, but their WNBA prop depth and prices are unpublished, quote-only, or unverified. Pre-2023 WNBA prop history looks largely unavailable off the shelf.

### Cited Findings
**The Odds API** (the-odds-api.com)
- WNBA markets include **player props (US bookmakers only)**. Featured markets (h2h/spreads/totals) use the odds endpoint. Player props and other additional markets use the **event-odds endpoint**. — [The Odds API WNBA page](https://the-odds-api.com/sports-odds-data/wnba-odds.html); [alt WNBA page](https://the-odds-api.com/sports/wnba-odds.html)
- Historical WNBA odds are available **on paid plans only**. The WNBA page says featured-market history starts **May 2022** and other markets **May 2023**. — [The Odds API WNBA page](https://the-odds-api.com/sports-odds-data/wnba-odds.html)
- The historical docs say "additional markets, including **player props**, period markets and more, is available from **May 3rd, 2023**." Snapshots come at **5-minute intervals**. The API returns the nearest snapshot at or before the requested timestamp. Quota cost is **10 per region per market** for the historical endpoint. — [The Odds API Historical Odds Data](https://the-odds-api.com/historical-odds-data/)
- Plan tiers (third-party directory, 2026, **unverified**): free Starter with 500 credits/month; **20K credits $30/mo; 100K $59/mo; 5M $119/mo; 15M $249/mo**. — [sportsapis.dev review](https://sportsapis.dev/apis/the-odds-api). Billing: paid plans bill immediately, renew monthly, and can be cancelled anytime. — [The Odds API FAQ](https://the-odds-api.com/manage/faqs.html)
- A competitor blog's credit example: a live call with 3 markets × 2 regions costs 6 credits, and the same historical call costs 60. The free tier hard-stops at zero. — [OddsPapi blog (competitor, unverified)](https://oddspapi.io/blog/?p=2967)

**SportsGameOdds**
- Its WNBA page lists player props and game props, pre-game and live, "across all major WNBA games." — [SportsGameOdds WNBA](https://sportsgameodds.com/leagues/wnba-odds-api)
- Its FAQ claims historical pre-match and in-play odds across major sports and books, **including closing lines, alternate spreads/totals, and player prop outcomes**. Pricing tiers run "Free to Pro." Dollar amounts were not found, and it is unconfirmed whether WNBA props are in the archive. — [SportsGameOdds FAQ](https://sportsgameodds.com/sportsgameodds-faq/); [comparison page (vendor)](https://sportsgameodds.com/compare/the-odds-api)

**SportsDataIO**
- WNBA docs say betting odds older than **30 days move into a Historical API data warehouse** (see its Historical Data Guide). Access is restricted and requires contacting sales. No pricing was found. — [SportsDataIO WNBA API docs](https://sportsdata.io/developers/api-documentation/wnba)

**OddsJam**
- API pricing is **quote-only/sales form**. — [sportsapis.dev OddsJam review](https://sportsapis.dev/apis/odds-jam); [OddsPapi (competitor)](https://oddspapi.io/blog/?p=2860)
- It reportedly advertises a "Full Historical Odds Database" with closing and opening odds. The directory review says it lacks **Pinnacle/Bet365**. Both claims are unverified. — [OddsPapi (competitor)](https://oddspapi.io/blog/?p=2860); [sportsapis.dev](https://sportsapis.dev/apis/odds-jam)

**OpticOdds**
- **April 2026:** OpticOdds added WNBA player-prop lines generated by **The Crowd's Line AI's in-house models (not market consensus)**, with probability distributions and "confidence-adjusted vig." — [AGBrief, Apr 20 2026](https://agbrief.com/news/world/20/04/2026/opticodds-strengthens-wnba-portfolio-with-ai-driven-prop-pricing-via-tcl-ai/); [European Gaming, Apr 20 2026](https://europeangaming.eu/portal/latest-news/2026/04/20/202171/opticodds-expands-wnba-player-props-via-the-crowds-line-ai-integration/); [OpticOdds blog](https://opticodds.com/blog/opticodds-partner-with-tcl)
- Historical odds go back "to when it began collecting" for each sport or book, with "several years" for major leagues and tier-one books. WNBA depth is not published (contact sales). — [OpticOdds historical odds](https://opticodds.com/es/historical-odds); [OpticOdds basketball](https://opticodds.com/sports/basketball); [developer docs](https://developer.opticodds.com/)
- A generic directory estimates comprehensive player-props API access at **$200–500/month**. This is not vendor-specific and is unverified. — [sportsapis.dev player props](https://sportsapis.dev/player-props-api)

**Others (vendor/competitor claims, unverified)**
- OddsPapi claims free historical odds and WNBA player-points props. — [OddsPapi WNBA blog](https://oddspapi.io/blog/?p=3102); [OddsPapi WNBA](https://oddspapi.io/sports/basketball/wnba)
- Odds-API.io documents a bulk `/v3/historical/closing-lines` endpoint. — [Odds-API.io docs](https://docs.odds-api.io/guides/historical)

**Official betting data (context)**
- NBA betting-data deals with **Sportradar and Genius Sports** (2018, later extended as multiyear renewals) cover official betting data for **all NBA and WNBA games**. Rights are **non-exclusive**, and the data goes to licensed US operators and platform providers. — [NBA PR (2018)](https://pr.nba.com/nba-betting-data-partnerships-sportradar-genius); [NBA PR (extension)](https://pr.nba.com/nba-sportradar-genius-sports-group-partnerships); [CNBC 2018](https://www.cnbc.com/2018/11/28/nba-strikes-betting-data-deals-with-sportradar-genius-sports.html)

### Inferences
- **Cost sketch (inference, using the unverified tier prices above and the documented 10-credit cost):** One historical snapshot of 5 prop markets in 1 region for 1 game costs about 50 credits. The 2026 season had 330 regular-season games ([CBS Sports](https://mauth.cbssports.com/wnba/news/wnba-2026-schedule-release-30th-season-begin-may-8-44-games-per-team/)). Ten snapshots per game (open to close) would be about 165K credits per season. That fits in one month of the ~$119/5M tier, so roughly **$120–250 one-off** could pull 2023–2026 WNBA prop history from The Odds API. Event-list calls and exact per-event historical costs need confirming.
- Usable **closing-line** archives for WNBA props realistically start in **2023** (The Odds API). With 44-game seasons, that is a small sample of roughly 3.5 seasons for calibrating a Benter-style model blended with market prices.
- OpticOdds' April 2026 WNBA props are **model-generated lines**, not book prices. They should not be treated as a market consensus or closing line in backtests.
- Without Pinnacle-style sharp anchors for WNBA props (none documented), "closing line" benchmarks will be US-retail consensus. That is a weaker efficiency benchmark than in major markets.

### Gaps
- Official current pricing pages for The Odds API, SportsGameOdds, OddsJam, OpticOdds and SportsDataIO could not be fetched. All dollar figures are third-party or estimates.
- Which books and specific WNBA prop market keys (points/rebounds/assists/threes/PRA, alt lines) The Odds API archives was not confirmed.
- No source confirmed WNBA prop history before May 2023 from any vendor, or whether any archive includes Pinnacle or offshore WNBA props.
- DFS pick'em lines (PrizePicks/Underdog) as a historical proxy were not researched.

## 3. Is WNBA player tracking (Second Spectrum/Genius Sports) or detailed rotation data available to the public or for purchase?

### Takeaway
League-wide optical tracking (Second Spectrum/Genius Sports) began on **May 14, 2024**. It was framed as a team, front-office and media product, and **no public or retail access was found**. Rotation and lineup data, however, can be rebuilt freely from play-by-play: ESPN-sourced PBP via **wehoop** (2002 onward) and **Basketball-Reference** on/off and PBP pages (100% PBP coverage since 2016, live for 2026). Official betting-grade feeds go only to licensed B2B partners.

### Cited Findings
- **March 2024:** the WNBA signed a multi-year deal making Second Spectrum (Genius Sports) its optical tracking provider. It was the first US women's league with league-wide optical tracking, launching at the **2024 regular-season tip-off on May 14**. Arena camera arrays capture low-latency 3D player pose and ball data. — [NBA/WNBA PR](https://pr.nba.com/wnba-genius-sports-tracking-data); [WNBA.com](https://www.wnba.com/news/wnba-genius-sports-data-tracking); [Sportico 2024](https://www.sportico.com/leagues/basketball/2024/wnba-genius-sports-data-tracking-deal-1234769384/); [SportBusiness](https://tech.sportbusiness.com/news/genius-second-spectrum-named-wnba-ball-and-player-tracking-provider/)
- Metrics for **teams** include shot quality, shooter impact, paint touches, play-type efficiencies, points per chance, contest quality, defensive matchups, max speed and distance covered. The release frames this as coaching and front-office capability. The commissioner mentioned future "insights and media elements for fans," but **no fan data product was described**. — [NBA/WNBA PR](https://pr.nba.com/wnba-genius-sports-tracking-data); [AP via NBC New York](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/sports/wnba-optical-tracking-enhance-player-analysis/5198272/)
- Genius integrates the tracking data into its own insights and analytics engine. — [AP via Barchart](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/24569448/wnba-to-use-optical-tracking-to-enhance-player-analysis)
- **wehoop (sportsdataverse, R):** draws on **ESPN** for live play-by-play and box scores, with shot locations when available. The CRAN README lists WNBA full PBP for **2002–2023** (`wehoop::load_wnba_pbp()`); later seasons are likely covered via a "most recent season" helper but are unconfirmed. Its NEWS notes a **Basketball-Reference WNBA scraper back to 1997** that needs no key and respects BBRef's ~20 requests/min limit. — [wehoop README (CRAN)](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/wehoop/readme/README.html); [wehoop NEWS](https://sportsdataverse.r-universe.dev/wehoop/NEWS.txt)
- **Basketball-Reference WNBA on/off pages** are built from play-by-play. PBP coverage is "over 99.7% all-time (100% complete since 2016)," and 2026 pages exist (e.g., Golden State Valkyries 2026). Free to view, but its figures can differ from official totals. — [BBRef GSV 2026 on/off](https://www.basketball-reference.com/wnba/teams/GSV/2026_on-off.html); [BBRef ATL 2025 on/off](https://www.basketball-reference.com/wnba/teams/ATL/2025_on-off.html)
- Official NBA/WNBA betting data goes non-exclusively through **Sportradar and Genius Sports** to licensed operators and platform providers. — [NBA PR](https://pr.nba.com/nba-betting-data-partnerships-sportradar-genius)
- Commercial scrapers exist (e.g., an Apify "ESPN WNBA Scraper" actor). Their ToS status is unclear. — [Apify](https://apify.com/parseforge/espn-wnba-scraper)

### Inferences
- For props, the practically valuable "rotation" signals (stint lengths, substitution patterns, lineup minutes, foul-trouble exits, blowout pulls) can be **derived from PBP** at near-zero cost. Tracking adds shot quality, contest and paint-touch features that mainly improve efficiency estimates. They matter less than minutes and usage for counting-stat props.
- Since teams and official-data partners hold tracking and books get official feeds, a small modeler's edge has to come from **better use of public PBP** (minutes and role modeling, injury redistribution), not from data access.

### Gaps
- Whether wnba.com/stats or the WNBA Stats API now exposes any tracking-derived stats (speed, distance, touches, hustle) in 2025–2026 could not be confirmed.
- No retail or academic licensing path for WNBA Second Spectrum data was found. Genius/Sportradar B2B pricing is unpublished.
- The exact latest season in wehoop's WNBA PBP loader (2024–2026) was not confirmed.

## 4. What data and features do public WNBA projection models use, and which signals matter most for props?

### Takeaway
No public, documented WNBA-specific projection methodology was found (Her Hoop Stats, ETR or others). Documented DFS/prop practice (NBA) agrees on a **minutes-first** hierarchy: project minutes, then per-minute rates and usage (adjusted for teammate absences), then pace and game environment (team total/spread). Recent-minutes weighting and injury/role news are the main drivers.

### Cited Findings
- Stokastic (NBA DFS) sets the order: "project the minutes, adjust the rates, then find the games with the pace and the total to reward all of it." — [Stokastic NBA DFS research](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/nba-dfs/nba-dfs-research)
- One approach uses a decay function weighting the **last 10 games significantly more than the previous 50**. — [Stokastic, using projections](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/nba-dfs/how-to-use-nba-dfs-projections)
- RotoGrinders' minutes method blends season MPG with a **5-game recent sample** (recent ≈35% weight in the example). It also covers key inputs: minutes, usage, pace, and turnover-adjusted usage. — [RotoGrinders: Projecting Minutes](https://rotogrinders.com/lessons/projecting-minutes-1149307); [RotoGrinders: Key Inputs](https://rotogrinders.com/lessons/key-inputs-in-an-nba-projections-system-1144825)
- Pace and Vegas totals scale opportunity, so projections rise for all players in high-total games. — [Stokastic](https://www.stokastic.com/articles/nba-dfs/nba-dfs-research); [Quadratic NBA DFS projections](https://www.quadratichq.com/use-cases/nba-dfs-projections-crafting-accurate-player-values)
- RotoWire publishes projected minutes and lineup data products for basketball. — [RotoWire projected minutes explainer](https://www.rotowire.com/basketball/article/nba-projected-minutes-explained-fantasy-basketball-97473)
- Her Hoop Stats runs a Substack newsletter (>5,000 subscribers) on women's basketball analytics. Its subscription features and model inputs were not found. — [Her Hoop Stats newsletter](https://herhoopstats.substack.com/)
- **Structural WNBA context:** the 2026 season had **44 games per team, 15 teams (Toronto Tempo and Portland Fire debuted), 330 games**, plus a FIBA World Cup break from Sept 4–13. NBC reports a new CBA agreed March 18, 2026 (not independently confirmed). — [CBS Sports](https://mauth.cbssports.com/wnba/news/wnba-2026-schedule-release-30th-season-begin-may-8-44-games-per-team/); [NBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/wnba/wnba-key-dates-2026-season-draft-start-schedule/6484190/?amp=1); [Swish Appeal](https://www.swishappeal.com/wnba/70912/schedule-season-start-cba-may-8-44-games-fiba-world-cup-break-all-star-commissioners-cup-aces-winning-streak-champions-record-wings-fever-dream-sky-sun-lynx-sparks-liberty-tempo-fire-mystics-storm-mer)

### Inferences
- For WNBA props, the most leveraged signals are likely (1) **minutes distribution** (starter status, injury report, back-to-backs, blowout risk from spread), (2) **usage redistribution** when teammates are out, from on/off and lineup PBP, (3) **pace and team total** from the game market, and (4) team-specific **designation reliability**. Referee crew foul tendencies are a second-order minutes/FTA input, available about 9 a.m. ET.
- Short seasons, two 2026 expansion rosters and mid-season breaks (FIBA) mean thin samples and heavy roster churn. **Hierarchical/Bayesian shrinkage** and fast in-season updating should matter more than in the NBA.
- Market-implied information (team totals, spreads, other books' prop lines) should probably enter as features or be blended (Benter-style) rather than ignored, given likely retail-book inefficiency.

### Gaps
- No documented WNBA-specific methodology was found for Her Hoop Stats, ETR, FantasyLabs, SaberSim, Basketball-Reference projections or a "FiveThirtyEight-style" WNBA model.
- No published feature-importance study for WNBA props (e.g., minutes vs. usage vs. pace variance decomposition) was found.
- The effect of referee crews on WNBA fouls/FTA/minutes is unquantified in the sources found.

## 5. Legal and ethical limits: integrity rules on non-public information, league/insider policies, scraping and ToS, athlete privacy

### Takeaway
The bright line is **non-public information from insiders**. The October 2025 federal indictments (Rozier, Damon Jones) treated buying and betting on confidential NBA injury information as **wire-fraud conspiracy**, and several states explicitly bar betting on insider information. Public, official sources (injury reports, referee postings, beat reporters' public posts, licensed APIs) are fair game. Scraping is a contract and ToS risk rather than a clear crime: Meta v. Bright Data favored logged-off scraping of public data, but the ruling was fact-specific, and Sports Reference enforces rate limits. Player props themselves face growing regulatory restriction, and betting-related harassment of WNBA players is a live league and regulator concern. Athlete health and biometric data should not be sought.

### Cited Findings
**Insider information: criminal and regulatory exposure**
- **Oct 23, 2025:** federal indictments (EDNY) charged Terry Rozier, Damon Jones and others with **conspiracy to commit wire fraud and money laundering** (up to 20 years each). The indictment alleges Jones **sold confidential injury information about Lakers players to co-conspirators for betting** during the 2022–23 and 2023–24 seasons, and details five instances of non-public information sold for gambling. — [ESPN](https://espn.com/nba/story/_/id/48904034/); [Front Office Sports, Sept 2026](https://frontofficesports.com/article/nba-betting-scandal-far-beyond-terry-rozier-and-damon-jones/); [LA Mag](https://lamag.com/sports/feds-drop-stunning-nba-gambling-indictment-terry-rozier-charged-damon-jones-left-out/)
- A superseding indictment added **bribery in sporting contests and honest-services wire fraud conspiracy** against Rozier, naming the NBA and Hornets as victims. Rozier pleaded not guilty. Jones reportedly changed his plea to guilty (one source says April 28, 2026; dates conflict across sources). — [ESPN](https://espn.com/nba/story/_/id/48904034/); [News5 Cleveland](https://www.news5cleveland.com/sports/miami-heat-guard-terry-rozier-pleads-not-guilty-to-sports-betting-charges); [CTV](https://www.ctvnews.ca/sports/article/miami-heat-guard-terry-rozier-to-be-arraigned-in-new-york-court-over-sports-betting-scheme/)
- **April 2024:** the NBA banned Jontay Porter for life for, among other things, **disclosing confidential information to sports bettors**, limiting his own participation for betting purposes, and betting on NBA games. — [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nba/news/nba-bans-jontay-porter-for-life-after-investigation-reveals-raptors-forward-violated-league-gambling-rules/)
- **State law (2019 analysis):** only six states (Indiana, Massachusetts, New Jersey, New York, Tennessee, West Virginia) specifically addressed betting on insider information. Most states instead bar categories of insiders (players, coaches, referees). — [Brownstein Hyatt (BHFS) 2019](https://www.bhfs.com/insight/insider-betting-deep-threat-or-no-harm-no-foul)
- A 2021 Connecticut bill analysis describes barring **anyone with access to nonpublic confidential information** that could affect an event's outcome from wagering on it. — [CT OLR bill analysis](https://www.cga.ct.gov/2021/BA/PDF/2021HB-06512-R000389-BA.PDF)
- **Arizona (2025):** clearly bars persons tied to an event from using nonpublic info, but provisions covering the general public are "less prevalent and consistent," and "insider information" is undefined. — [Arizona State Law Journal, Feb 2025](https://arizonastatelawjournal.org/2025/02/18/arizonas-regulatory-gap-in-addressing-insider-information-in-sports-betting/)
- League rules supplement state law. The NFL threatens lifetime bans for "tipping," and NCAA rules bar providing information to people involved in wagering. — [Jackson Walker](https://www.jw.com/news/insights-sports-gambling/)

**WNBA player and league rules**
- WNBA CBA **Article XIV, Section 5(c)** prohibits players from wagering directly or indirectly, or attempting to wager, on WNBA games or any part of them. Penalties (fine, suspension, dismissal) are at the Commissioner's discretion. **July 2026:** the league deleted a social post showing Angel Reese and Paige Bueckers making a $400 wager, said it "missed the mark," and noted players get annual betting-policy training. AP reported no discipline. — [Fox News/OutKick](https://www.foxnews.com/outkick-sports/wnba-admits-controversial-betting-video-featuring-angel-reese-paige-bueckers-missed-mark.print); [The Post Millennial](https://thepostmillennial.com/wnba-posts-clip-showing-angel-reese-paige-bueckers-betting-on-outcome-of-game-then-deletes-it-after-realizing-thats-a-violation-of-their-own-rules)

**Scraping and terms of service**
- **Meta v. Bright Data (N.D. Cal., 2024):** summary judgment for Bright Data. Meta's terms governed users' "use" of its products, so **logged-off scraping of public data** (and selling it) was not a breach. Meta later dropped the suit. Commentators stress the ruling is **fact-specific** and does not bless all logged-off scraping. — [Farella Braun + Martel](https://www.fbm.com/data-analytics/publications/major-decision-affects-law-of-scraping-and-online-data-collection-meta-platforms-v-bright-data/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/393996/meta-abandons-lawsuit-against-bright-data-over-scr.html); [Zyte](https://www.zyte.com/blog/california-court-meta-ruling)
- **Sports Reference** rate-limits sessions to **20 requests/min** (10 for FBref/Stathead), with violators "jailed" for up to a day. It offers no API because its third-party data licenses bar offering downloads, and a separate Data Use page governs reuse. The policy page was last updated May 29, 2024. — [Sports-Reference bot traffic policy](https://www.sports-reference.com/bot-traffic.html)

**Athlete biometric and health data privacy**
- The NBA CBA bars in-game wearable use, makes practice use voluntary, bans using biometric data in contract negotiations ($250K fine) and has a joint oversight committee. A Pepperdine law review comment argues HIPAA, GINA and current CBAs give **insufficient protection** against gambling interests in athletes' biometric data. — [Pepperdine J. Bus. Entrepreneurship & Law](https://digitalcommons.pepperdine.edu/jbel/vol19/iss1/2); [IUPUI J. Legal Aspects of Sport](https://journals.iupui.edu/index.php/jlas/article/view/23894); [Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/thr-esq/is-betting-an-athletes-heart-rate-a-game-coming-broadcasting-guest-column-1303582)

### Inferences
- **Safe harbor in practice:** use only (1) official league publications (injury report, referee assignments, box scores and PBP), (2) **publicly posted** media and beat-reporter information after publication, (3) licensed or paid APIs under their terms, and (4) your own model outputs. Never solicit, buy or accept information from players, team staff, trainers, officials or league employees, even if it seems benign (e.g., "she tweaked something at shootaround"). The Jones/Rozier case shows prosecutors frame this as defrauding sportsbooks.
- Watching athletes' personal social accounts, travel or location, or medical details beyond official reports crosses into **privacy and harassment territory**. Given the 2026 harassment letter, a responsible modeler should avoid any data collection about players' private lives and never contact players about bets.
- **Scraping:** prefer official, open or licensed sources and respect rate limits and robots. Logged-in scraping of sportsbooks (or automated bet placement) likely violates their account terms and risks account closure and voided bets. This is an inference, since sportsbook ToS were not retrieved.
- **Regulatory risk to the strategy itself:** prop menus may shrink in some states (NY, OH, NJ). WNBA props tied to integrity-sensitive events (fouls, turnovers) are the most likely to disappear, so a model centered on points, rebounds and assists is more durable.
