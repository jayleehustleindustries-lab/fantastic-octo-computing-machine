# Pick'em App Economics, Rules and Restrictions (Dabble focus, with PrizePicks/Underdog comparisons) for $5 WNBA Prop Slips

Research date: 2026-10-10. Method note: page fetching (WebFetch/curl) was blocked in this environment (DNS/proxy failures), so all evidence below comes from search-engine result summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the pages in full. Treat exact multipliers as "as reported by the cited page around its publish date" and confirm in the app's bet slip before entering. A key structural point that runs through everything: **Dabble US (dabble.com / help.dabblefantasy.com) is a DFS operator; Dabble Australia (dabble.com.au / helpdesk.dabble.com.au) is a licensed bookmaker. Their payout tables and rules differ.** Many search results mix the two.

## 1. Dabble's current payout multipliers, flex/hedge/insurance options, and treatment of pushes and DNPs

### Takeaway
Dabble US All-In (every pick must hit) pays about 3x / 6x / 10x / 20x for 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 picks. That table is consistent across October 2026 sources, though sources disagree on the higher tiers (6-pick listed as 35x or 40x after a reported September 2026 rescale). Dabble Australia's official helpdesk lists a richer table (3.2x / 6.5x / 12x / 25x / 40x). Both Dabble products remove DNP legs and pay on the reduced entry, and refund the entry if fewer than 2 legs remain. A push (stat lands exactly on a whole-number line) is not a refund. On Dabble AU the pushed leg is dropped and the multiplier recalculated.

**Pushes (exact landing on a whole-number line)**
- Dabble AU: a push is when the result lands exactly on the projection (for example, projected 32 points and scores 32). Pushed legs are dropped and the multiplier recalculated; a 16-leg Hedge with 2 pushes becomes a 14-leg Hedge — [Dabble AU: How do pushes (ties) affect payouts](https://helpdesk.dabble.com.au/en/articles/13525043-how-do-pushes-ties-affect-payouts-in-dabble-betting)
- Dabble AU: on a 2-pick All-In, 1 win plus 1 push pays **1.5x**, and 1 loss plus 1 push pays nothing. "A push is not a refund." If all legs push, the entry may qualify for a refund — [Dabble AU pushes article](https://helpdesk.dabble.com.au/en/articles/13525043-how-do-pushes-ties-affect-payouts-in-dabble-betting)
- Dabble AU General Sport Rules treat void and push differently: a void on a 2-leg All-In is refunded, but a push is not — [Dabble AU General Sport Rules](https://helpdesk.dabble.com.au/en/articles/9783614-general-sport-rules)
- Third-party explanation: on a 3-pick All-In with one push, the pushed leg is grayed out, the stake stays on the remaining legs, and the payout drops to the 2-pick multiplier — [Ballislife: What does push mean on Dabble](https://ballislife.com/betting/reviews/dabble/what-does-push-mean/). **Caveat:** the same page's worked example uses multipliers that match no current table.

**DNP / voided players**
- Dabble AU Pick'em: if a player takes no part in the game at any point, the leg is voided and removed, and the multiplier adjusts to the remaining legs. A player who plays but records zero stats does not void the leg — [Dabble AU Pick'em help](https://helpdesk.dabble.com.au/en/articles/10254422-pick-em)
- Dabble AU voids chart: a Pick'em All-In is refunded when it drops below 2 legs, when all legs are void, or when all players from one team are void (it no longer meets the two-team requirement) — [Dabble AU: How voids work across bet types](https://helpdesk.dabble.com.au/en/articles/15573843-how-voids-work-across-different-bet-types)
- Dabble AU Injury Protection generally does not cover Pick'em or Over/Under markets — [Dabble AU Injury Protection Policy](https://helpdesk.dabble.com.au/en/articles/9783647-dabble-au-injury-protection-policy)
- Dabble US (per Legal Sports Report review): a DNP leg is voided. A 3-pick with one player injured before the game is graded as a 2-pick and pays 3x — [Legal Sports Report Dabble review](https://www.legalsportsreport.com/dfs-sites/dabble/)

**Comparable apps, for context**
- PrizePicks: DNPs and "reboots" remove the pick and revert the lineup to a smaller size. **Ties are not removed; they revert the payout down one level.** If the remaining players are all on one team, the entry is refunded automatically — [PrizePicks payouts page](https://www.prizepicks.com/resources/prizepicks-payouts)
- PrizePicks: an entry that reverts because of a DNP or tie and then hits fully counts as a full win (for example, a 3-pick Flex reverting to a 2-pick Power) — [PrizePicks on X](https://twitter.com/PrizePicks/status/1536853128871649280)
- Underdog: a tied projection is treated as a voided selection, and the payout adjusts to the remaining active picks (a 5-pick Flex with one void becomes a 4-pick Flex) — [Underdog help: tie (voided) selection](https://help.underdogsports.com/en/articles/10982402-what-happens-in-the-event-of-a-tie-voided-selection); [OddsJam: pushes on Underdog](https://oddsjam.com/betting-education/how-do-pushes-work-on-underdog)
- Underdog's current help center lists standard payouts of 3.5x (2 picks), 6.5x (3 picks) and 20x (5 picks). Older third-party guides list 3x / 6x — [Underdog help: Standard & Flex payouts](https://help.underdogsports.com/en/articles/13780101-pick-em-standard-flex-entry-payouts)
- PrizePicks: 6-pick Power pays 37.5x and a 6/6 Flex pays 25x; partial tiers include 3-of-5 Flex at 0.4x. PrizePicks says payouts can vary by promotion and by state — [PrizePicks Payouts Explained (help)](https://intercom.help/prizepicks/en/articles/9047668-payouts-explained). PrizePicks also launched 2-pick Flex, which pays out with only one correct pick — [PrizePicks Playbook](https://www.prizepicks.com/playbook-article/2-pick-flex-plays-on-prizepicks-hit-one-pick-get-paid)

### Inferences
- For WNBA props on Dabble, **whole-number lines carry push risk, and a push is not a refund**. On a 2-pick, one win plus one push on Dabble AU pays 1.5x (a net +$2.50 on $5), not the 3x of a full win. Half-point lines remove this risk. When a model favors a side on a whole-number line, the exact-landing probability has to be priced in separately.
- DNP is generally benign: the slip shrinks to a smaller All-In. A 2-pick with one DNP collapses below the 2-leg minimum and is refunded. A 3-pick with one DNP becomes a 2-pick at 3x, which has the worst per-leg break-even (see Q2), so a DNP silently moves the slip onto a worse price.
- The "two teams" requirement matters for WNBA's small slates. If voids leave only one team, the entry is refunded, not paid.

## 2. Break-even per-leg hit rates by slip size and house edge compared with a -110 sportsbook bet (52.4%)

### Takeaway
On Dabble US's 3x / 6x / 10x / 20x table, each leg must hit **57.7% (2-pick), 55.0% (3-pick), 56.2% (4-pick) and 54.9% (5-pick)** to break even, against 52.4% for a -110 single. At true 50/50 legs, the house edge is 25% on 2- and 3-pick slips and 37.5% on 4- to 6-pick slips (40x six-pick), against 4.5% on a -110 bet. The 2-pick 3x is the worst value per leg, the equivalent of laying about -137 on each leg.

### Cited Findings
- Third-party math for PrizePicks: a 2-pick Power at 3x prices out around -137 and needs about 57.7% per leg; 5- and 6-pick Flex need just over 54%; 3-pick Flex is worst at almost 60% — [Action Network: PrizePicks flex or power math](https://www.actionnetwork.com/education/prizepicks-flex-play-or-power-play-math); [BettingPros: picking the right slip size](https://www.bettingpros.com/articles/how-to-make-money-on-prizepicks-picking-the-right-slip-size-props/)
- Payout inputs are from the sources cited in Q1: Dabble US 3/6/10/20/35-or-40x ([CBS](https://www.cbssports.com/betting/news/dabble/), [FantasyLabs](https://www.fantasylabs.com/articles/dabble-promo-code/)); Dabble AU 3.2/6.5/12/25/40x ([Dabble AU rules](https://helpdesk.dabble.com.au/en/articles/9783614-general-sport-rules)); Underdog 3.5/6.5/20x ([Underdog help](https://help.underdogsports.com/en/articles/13780101-pick-em-standard-flex-entry-payouts)); PrizePicks 3x 2-pick and 37.5x 6-pick ([PrizePicks help](https://intercom.help/prizepicks/en/articles/9047668-payouts-explained))

### Inferences
These tables are computed by me. Formulas: All-In break-even per leg p = (1/M)^(1/n). EV per $1 at p = M·pⁿ − 1. Hedge/Flex break-even is solved numerically with binomial outcomes. All of this assumes independent legs and ignores pushes, voids, boosts and promos.

**All-In / Power break-even per leg**

| Product (payout source) | 2-pick | 3-pick | 4-pick | 5-pick | 6-pick |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Dabble US (3/6/10/20/40x) | 57.7% | 55.0% | 56.2% | 54.9% | 54.1% (40x) / 55.3% (35x) |
| Dabble AU (3.2/6.5/12/25/40x) | 55.9% | 53.6% | 53.7% | 52.5% | 54.1% |
| Underdog (3.5/6.5/–/20x) | 53.4% | 53.6% | n/a | 54.9% | n/a |
| PrizePicks Power (3x / 37.5x) | 57.7% | n/a | n/a | n/a | 54.7% |
| Sportsbook -110 single (or -110 parlay, 3.645x for 2 legs) | 52.4% per leg | | | | |

**House edge if every leg were a true coin flip (50%):** Dabble US 2-pick −25%, 3-pick −25%, 4-pick −37.5%, 5-pick −37.5%, 6-pick −37.5% (40x) or −45% (35x). Dabble AU 2-pick −20%, 3-pick −19%, 5-pick −22%. Underdog 2-pick −12.5%. A -110 single is −4.5%.

**Per-leg American-odds equivalent** of the price you are laying: Dabble US 2-pick about −137, 3-pick about −122, 4-pick about −128, 5-pick about −122, 6-pick (40x) about −118.

**Hedge/Flex break-even per leg:** Dabble US 3-pick Hedge (2.5x / 1.25x) 57.4%. Dabble AU 3-leg Hedge (3x / 1.2x) 55.3%, 4-leg (5x / 2x) 53.9%, 5-leg (10x / 2x / 0.5x) 53.7%, 6-leg (25x / 2.5x / 0.5x) 53.2%. A PrizePicks-style 6-pick Flex (25x / 2x / 0.4x) is about 54.2%. Hedge trades slightly worse or similar break-evens for lower variance.

- What this means in practice: **a model that only beats 52.4% (a "-110-beating" model) loses money on every Dabble slip size.** Each leg needs roughly 55% or better for 3- and 5-pick slips, and close to 58% for 2-pick slips. The edge has to come from soft or stale pick'em lines, for example lines that disagree with sharp-book devigged prices, rather than from small modeling edges.
- Correlation: the formula assumes independent legs. Positively correlated legs, such as a player's over paired with a same-game teammate's over, raise the true all-hit probability. That is why Dabble adjusts payouts for same-game combinations (see Q3), and the edge may already be priced out.

## 3. Do Dabble/PrizePicks/Underdog restrict or ban winning ("sharp") players? Correlated-pick rules, regulator actions 2024–2026, and legality by jurisdiction (US states, Australia)

### Takeaway
None of the operators publishes a "we limit winners" policy, but all reserve broad discretion. Dabble US's terms let it change entry limits and min/max entry amounts in its sole discretion without notice. PrizePicks reserves the right to limit accounts for "other relevant purposes". User reports describe pick'em restrictions after winning. Dabble restricts correlated picks: same-game combinations reduce the multiplier, players are usually limited to one stat per entry, and every entry needs at least two teams. Regulation is a state-by-state patchwork. Several states (NY, MI, FL, AZ, AR and others) have forced house-banked pick'em out or into peer-to-peer formats. California's AG declared all pick'em illegal in July 2025, and Alabama forced format changes in June 2026. Dabble US reports about 29–31 states plus DC, with formats varying by state. In Australia, Dabble's pick'em is a licensed NT bookmaker product; offshore DFS-style apps are not licensed there.

## 5. Bankroll math for a $15–$40 bankroll on $5 slips: risk of ruin with a small edge, fractional Kelly for multi-leg slips, and responsible-gambling guidance

### Takeaway
With $5 flat slips on a $15–$40 bankroll, **ruin is the most likely outcome even with a real edge**. For example, with $40 on 2-pick 3x slips at a strong 60% per leg (+8% EV per slip), there is about a 39% chance of busting within 100 slips and about 53% over 1,000. At break-even it is about 57% within 100 slips. Kelly sizing says a $5 slip is roughly 3–25 times too big for these bankrolls. Quarter-Kelly at realistic edges implies bankrolls of several hundred to a few thousand dollars per $5 slip.

### Cited Findings
- The Kelly criterion maximizes long-run log growth. Betting more than the Kelly fraction increases risk and drawdowns — [Wikipedia: Kelly criterion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kelly_criterion)
- Full Kelly produces large drawdowns. Fractional Kelly can keep drawdowns within a chosen limit while staying close to optimal growth — [arXiv 1801.01948](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.01948)
- Ruin can be modeled as an absorbing boundary where the bankroll becomes too small to continue; fractional Kelly's effect on returns and ruin has been analyzed — [Entropy (2024), via DOAJ](https://doaj.org/article/d17ac3508b694975bad800ace4c4d8a6)
- Karl Whelan: the higher variance of parlays often offsets the extra expected return from stacking edges, and the typical 5-leg parlay cannot be justified by having an edge — [Karl Whelan: The Parlay Puzzle](https://www.karlwhelan.com/?p=2466)
- Practitioner guidance is to use fractional Kelly (often 25% or less) because true win probabilities are estimated with error, and that error compounds across parlay legs — [TopEndSports Kelly calculator](https://www.topendsports.com/sports-betting/tools/kelly-criterion-calculator/); [TheStatsAPI Kelly tool](https://www.thestatsapi.com/tools/kelly-criterion-calculator)
- Dabble US offers Safer Gaming tools, including deposit limits and user-set limits through account settings or customer service — [Dabble US Safer Gaming](https://help.dabblefantasy.com/en/collections/14313619-safer-gaming); [Dabble US T&C](https://help.dabblefantasy.com/en/articles/12051129-general-dabble-terms-and-conditions)
- Dabble AU offers BetStop self-exclusion and account closure in-app (Settings) — [dabble.com.au](https://www.dabble.com.au/)
- Promo context: the Dabble US sign-up offer is "Play $5, Get $50 in Bonus Cash" (it requires verification and a qualifying $5 real-money entry), and Rebound gives Bonus Cash on losing 4+ pick All-In entries — [Dabble US help: sign-up offer](https://help.dabblefantasy.com/en/articles/16074109-sign-up-offer-play-5-get-50-00-in-bonus-cash); [Dabble US help: Rebound](https://help.dabblefantasy.com/en/articles/13680649-rebound)

- **Main implication:** a $5 slip is 12.5% of a $40 bankroll and 33% of a $15 bankroll. Even at an excellent 60% per leg, full Kelly on a 2-pick is only 4% of bankroll. At $40, a $5 slip is about 3x full Kelly, or about 12x quarter-Kelly. Over-betting Kelly by 2x or more drives long-run growth to zero or below, which is why the ruin probabilities above stay high even with a positive edge (Wikipedia, cited above).
- More legs lower the per-leg break-even (5-pick 54.9% vs 2-pick 57.7%), but they also cut Kelly sizes and raise short-run ruin sharply: 5-pick ruin is higher than 2- or 3-pick at the same bankroll, even with much higher EV. On these payouts, **3-pick 6x is the best trade-off between break-even (55.0%) and variance**. A 2-pick 3x needs a 57.7%+ leg rate before it is even neutral.
- Per-leg probability estimates carry model error. If a model says 58% and the truth is 55%, a 2-pick at 3x goes from +0.9% to −9.2% EV. Errors compound multiplicatively on multi-leg slips, which supports quarter-Kelly or smaller and a minimum edge buffer of several points over break-even before entering.
- Practical small-stakes guidance (my inference from the math):
  - Treat a $15–$40 balance as a test or entertainment budget, not a bankroll.
  - Use the smallest entry the app allows (Rebound applies to entries of $1 or more, so $1 entries exist on Dabble US). This brings stake/bankroll toward Kelly-consistent levels.
  - Track closing-line or market-implied probability for each leg to validate edge before scaling.
  - Set a deposit limit in-app; on Dabble AU, BetStop is available.
  - Bonus cash (the $50 sign-up offer, Rebound) can outweigh the expected edge at these stakes, but its playthrough and withdrawal terms were not verified.
